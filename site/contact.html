Contact

Talk to the team behind AAA Tracker.

Tell us what you're tracking and where your conversions come from. We'll show you the shortest path to a campaign whose numbers reconcile with your ad platform.

What happens next

You write to a person, not a ticket queue. Here is the shape of the conversation.

  • We reply within one business day — usually sooner during the working week.
  • We'll ask for your traffic source, your offer, and how your network posts conversions, so the walkthrough runs on your setup rather than a demo account.
  • You'll leave with a working tracking link and a report you can compare, line for line, against what the ad platform says it spent.

How to reach us

Email is the fastest route. It reaches someone who can answer a tracking question on the first reply.

Before you write

A few questions we get first.

What should I include in my message?

Your traffic source, the offer or network you're running, and how conversions come back to you — postback, server-side API, or a CSV at the end of the day. If you already have tracking links or a campaign running somewhere else, describe the shape of it. The more of your setup you put in the first email, the less back-and-forth there is.

Can you migrate my existing campaigns?

Yes. We'll take your campaigns, offers, landers and domains from an export or from the platform's API, map the fields, and check the numbers against a source you already trust before anything goes live. Migration is usually a single working session.

Do you offer onboarding for agencies?

We do. Agency onboarding covers multiple workspaces or clients, shared team access, and the reporting views you'll hand to a client. Tell us how many workspaces you run and who needs access, and we'll scope it on the call.
