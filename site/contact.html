Before you write

A few questions we get first.

01What should I include in my message?

Your traffic source, the offer or network you’re running, and how conversions come back to you — postback, server-side API, or a CSV at the end of the day. If you already have tracking links or a campaign running somewhere else, describe the shape of it.

02Can you migrate my existing campaigns?

Yes. We’ll take your campaigns, offers, landers and domains from an export or from the platform’s API, map the fields, and check the numbers against a source you already trust before anything goes live. Migration is usually a single working session.

03Do you offer onboarding for agencies?

We do. Agency onboarding covers multiple workspaces or clients, shared team access, and the reporting views you’ll hand to a client. Tell us how many workspaces you run and who needs access, and we’ll scope it on the call.

04Can I try it before I commit?

Yes — start a 14-day free trial with no credit card. You get the whole tracker for 14 days, then pick the paid plan that fits. Say so in your message and we’ll get your workspace set up.
