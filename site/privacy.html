Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy covers two things: the marketing site at aaatracker.website, and the AAA Tracker product you sign in to. It explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the controls you have over it. We’ve written it in plain language on purpose.

Who we are

AAA Tracker provides campaign tracking and analytics for performance marketers. Where this policy says “we” or “us”, it means the team that operates AAA Tracker. If you have a question that this page doesn’t answer, the contact details are at the bottom.

What we collect

  • Account data — the name, email address and role of each person who signs in to a workspace.
  • Workspace data you create — campaigns, offers, landing pages, domains, flows and the settings around them.
  • Tracking data processed on your behalf — clicks, conversions and cost records, together with the identifiers your tracking sends us, such as click id, sub-ids, user agent and IP address.
  • Billing and contact records — if you email us or book a walkthrough, we keep that correspondence and the details you give us.

Why we process it

We process data for four reasons:

  • to provide and secure the service — running your account, keeping it available, and defending it against abuse and fraud;
  • to attribute clicks and conversions on your instruction — matching the traffic you send to the conversions your network reports;
  • to send the reports and alerts you configure — scheduled emails, threshold alerts and notifications your workspace asks for;
  • to answer you — responding to a question, a support request or a walkthrough.

Our role

For the tracking data you collect on your own campaigns, you are the controller and we are the processor. We handle that data only on your documented instructions, and we don’t use it for our own purposes. A data processing agreement is available on request — ask us through the contact page.

For the marketing site and your account, we are the controller: we decide how that data is used, and this policy describes it.

Cookies

The product uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your session. The marketing site does not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We don’t ask for consent for cookies we don’t use.

Retention

Tracking data is kept for the retention window configured on each workspace, and is then pruned automatically. Account data is kept while the account is active. After a workspace closes we keep only what we are required to keep for billing, tax and legal records, and delete the rest.

Security

Platform tokens are encrypted at rest, every record is isolated to its workspace, and access is controlled by role. The measures are described in more detail on the security page.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, export it, delete it, or stop processing it, and you can object to processing you don’t agree with. To exercise any of these, contact us. If you are a workspace user, you can also export your settings and clear tracking history from inside the product, without waiting on us.

Third parties

We use a small number of sub-processors to run the service — hosting for the application and database, and an email provider for delivery — and each receives only what a task needs. When you connect an ad platform, that platform receives the events you choose to send. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is material, we’ll announce it in the product or by email before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to exercise a right, can go to hello@aaatracker.website or through the contact page. We reply within one business day.
