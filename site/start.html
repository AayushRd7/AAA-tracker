Whichever you choose

Three objects to go live.

The setup is the same model media buyers already think in — an afternoon, not a project.

  1. Connect the source

    Pick your traffic source — or add it — and the tracking link is generated with that source’s real macros already in place.

  2. Add offers and landers

    Point at an offer URL or upload a landing page, then give the network its postback URL. Secret key and IP allowlist are optional.

  3. Read the truth

    Every click, conversion, cost and postback lands in one ledger — with profit, CR, EPC and ROI per row, updated as it happens.
