Features

The routing engine, and everything around it.

A tracker is only as good as the decisions it makes in the half-second before a redirect. Here is what AAA Tracker decides with — and what it does with the answer.

01 — ROUTING

Flow filters

30+ fields with AND/OR groups: geo, device model, OS and version, browser, language, ISP, connection type, IP ranges and IPv6, referrer, keyword, sub-ids and user agent.

02 — ROUTING

Distribution modes

Send traffic by weight, by position, or let the optimizer move the weights for you. Repeat visitors stay on the variant they were first given.

03 — ROUTING

Actions

302 redirect, JavaScript and meta-refresh redirects, form POST, open in iframe, serve remote content without redirecting, show HTML, return 404, or do nothing.

04 — ROUTING

Limits & overflow

Daily conversion caps per offer with automatic overflow to a chained backup, plus per-flow caps by hour, day or total.

05 — ROUTING

Scheduling

Dayparting per flow: days, hours and timezone. Outside the window the flow simply doesn't match.

06 — ROUTING

Fallback & referrer

A global fallback URL for unmatched clicks, and referrer hiding for networks that reject a straight redirect.

07 — TESTING

Landers & offers

Unlimited variants per flow, split by weight, with signed-cookie binding so a visitor always sees the same variant.

08 — TESTING

Multi-step funnels

Chain lander → upsell → thank-you with per-step visits, click-outs, conversions and drop-off.

09 — TESTING

Impressions

Track views as well as clicks, and see view-through conversions in the same report.

10 — TRACKING

Postbacks

Query-style conversion URLs with macros for click id, payout, status, transaction id and any custom field. Secret key and IP allowlist protection are optional.

11 — TRACKING

De-duplication

A 60-second window plus transaction ids stop a retried postback from becoming a second sale.

12 — TRACKING

Retroactive updates

Change a status or payout after the fact — rejected, hold, upsell — and every report follows.

13 — TRACKING

Server-side APIs

Conversion API integrations push events straight to the ad platform, independent of the browser.

14 — TRACKING

Click API

Post a click from an app or webview and get the routing decision back as JSON — no redirect required.

15 — TRACKING

Clickless conversions

When a click id was never stored, attribute the conversion by sub-id or transaction id instead of losing it.

16 — COST

Ad-platform cost sync

Daily spend, impressions and clicks pulled per campaign and folded into the tracker's cost. Dry-run first, so nothing is written until you say so.

17 — COST

Manual & API cost updates

Set cost per campaign by hand, or let a cost-update API call adjust it — no double counting.

18 — COST

Profit, ROI, EPC

Computed per row, per day, per source, per offer and per placement — with the currency the workspace runs on.

19 — PROTECTION

Fraud scoring

A 0–100 score on every click from proxy, datacenter, headless-browser and behavioural signals, plus a live feed of what it caught.

20 — PROTECTION

Bot filter

Built-in verified-crawler lists, editable global user-agent and IP blocklists, and a per-campaign switch.

21 — PROTECTION

Per-campaign shields

IP, referrer and user-agent allowlists with a choose-your-own response: blank page, 404, or fall through to a safe flow.

22 — PROTECTION

Honeypots

Invisible trap links flag automated visitors permanently, so one detection protects every future click.

23 — AUTOMATION

Rules

Condition-based automation: when a metric crosses a threshold, pause a flow, disable a campaign, or alert a channel.

24 — AUTOMATION

Optimizer

Every 15 minutes, flow weights are re-balanced toward the best performer by CR, EPC, profit or revenue — with an exploration floor so nothing is starved.

25 — AUTOMATION

Flow monitoring

Offers, landers and redirect targets are HTTP-checked on a schedule; a dead destination is taken out of rotation and you get told.

26 — AUTOMATION

Anomaly insights

Detectors watch for revenue stops, cost spikes and conversion collapse, and surface a prioritised list with the campaign to look at.

27 — REPORTING

Breakdowns

Slice by campaign, source, offer, lander, country, device, browser, OS, sub-id and more — multi-level, with totals.

28 — REPORTING

Saved views

Save dimensions, columns, filters and sorting as a report you can reopen or share with a read-only public link.

29 — REPORTING

Scheduled email

Mail a snapshot daily or weekly at a chosen hour — the daily account summary or any saved report.

30 — REPORTING

Logs

Clicks, conversions, postbacks, click forwarding, internal postbacks and cost changes — each with the fields you need to debug a discrepancy.

31 — REPORTING

Copilot

Ask a plain-language question about your own aggregates and get a grounded answer with the numbers behind it.

32 — REPORTING

Health centre

One screen for the data plane: recent delivery failures, loop heartbeats, and the counts that tell you the tracker is alive.
