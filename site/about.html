About

Built by performance marketers.

We built the tracker we wanted: fast, honest about attribution, and clear about where every dollar went. AAA Tracker is that tool — the same one we run our own campaigns on.

Why we built it

Money leaks in the gaps between systems.

Run paid traffic for long enough and you learn where the money disappears. The ad platform reports one number, the network reports another, and the tracker in the middle quietly disagrees with both — or loses a conversion entirely and never says so.

Those gaps are not abstract. They are the click that arrived before the record was written. The postback that fired twice. The spend pulled from the wrong timezone. The placement that looked fine until you sliced it by country. Each one costs real budget, and none of them show up until the month is over.

We wanted one place where spend, clicks, conversions and payouts agree with each other — so the answer to "where did the money go" is a report, not an investigation.

  • Decisions in the path of the click — routing happens before the redirect, not in a nightly job.
  • Spend and revenue in one ledger — cost sync folded in with clicks and conversions, not stitched together in a spreadsheet.
  • Discrepancies you can debug — the raw click, postback and cost logs are there when two numbers disagree.
  • Honest about what it can't see — no inflated attribution, no silent guesses presented as fact.
Principles

What we believe.

Four positions that shape every decision in the product — including the ones that cost us an easier sale.

01 — ATTRIBUTION

Attribution is a promise

If a sale happened, it should be credited. Conversions are matched against the click record itself, retried postbacks are de-duplicated, and clickless conversions are attributed by sub-id instead of being lost.

02 — DATA

Your data is yours

Every record is isolated to your workspace and platform tokens are encrypted at rest. You can export your data or delete it on demand, and you never have to ask us for permission to leave.

03 — SPEED

Speed is a feature

Routing decisions happen in the path of the click, in the half-second before the redirect. A tracker that adds latency to every visit is costing you conversions while it reports on them.

04 — HONESTY

No dark patterns

No per-event metering surprises, no hostage features held back to force an upgrade, no billing that grows just because a campaign worked. Pricing should be as clear as the reporting.
