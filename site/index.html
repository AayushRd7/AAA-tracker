The foundation

Your ad platforms can’t optimise data they never received.

Pixels miss conversions, redirects drop parameters and iOS eats the cookie. Nothing looks broken — the report still adds up. So the platform optimises harder toward the wrong thing.

  • Server-side capture — immune to ad blockers and browser limits

Questions

The things buyers ask first.

What exactly does AAA Tracker do?

It sits between your traffic and your offers. It decides which offer or landing page each visitor sees, records the click, records the conversion when the network posts it back, pulls your ad spend in, and reports profit per campaign, source, offer and placement.

How much does it cost?

Self-hosted is free forever under the source-available licence. Hosted plans start at $0 for 50,000 tracked events a month and scale to $499 for 20 million — one meter, no add-on store, and cost sync plus server-side conversion APIs included on every paid plan. See Pricing.

Will it work with my traffic sources and networks?

Yes. Sources and networks carry their own pass-through macros, so campaign, placement, keyword and sub-id data is captured without hand-built parameters. If a source isn’t in the list, adding one takes about a minute.

How does it handle iOS and cookie restrictions?

Clicks are recorded server-side and conversions come back as postbacks or server-side conversion API calls, so attribution doesn’t depend on a third-party cookie surviving.

Is my data separate from other customers?

Yes. Every record is scoped to your workspace, and platform tokens are encrypted at rest. See Security for the detail.

Do I need to be technical to set it up?

No. You add a source, an offer and a campaign, then copy the tracking link. Landing pages can be uploaded or pulled from a URL, and ad platforms connect through OAuth rather than a pasted token.
