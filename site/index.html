Why trackers get abandoned

The numbers never quite add up.

You buy traffic on one dashboard, convert it somewhere else, and reconcile it in a spreadsheet. Every gap between those three is money you can't see.

Spend and revenue live apart

Ad platforms report spend; networks report payouts. Until both land in the same row, every ROAS figure is a guess.

Attribution gets lost

Redirects drop parameters, ITP eats cookies, and postbacks arrive before the click is written. Unattributed conversions look like profit you never earned.

Bots and fraud pay for themselves

Datacenter IPs, headless browsers and competitor crawlers chew through budget while your reports show healthy traffic.

Routing engine

Decide where each click goes — precisely.

Filters stack in groups with AND/OR logic, so "US desktop Chrome on Black Friday, except datacenter IPs" is one flow, not five.

  • 30+ filter fields — geography, device, OS and version, browser, ISP, connection type, referrer, keyword, sub-ids, user agent, bot status.
  • Real operators — is / is not, contains, regex, CIDR ranges and IPv6.
  • Multiple actions — redirect, form POST, iframe, serve content, show HTML, 404, or do nothing.
  • Fallback URL when nothing matches, and referrer hiding when the network demands it.

Attribution you can defend

Conversions that arrive before the click still get credited.

Postbacks are matched against the click record itself, not a cache that may not have been written yet — so a fast network can't turn a sale into an orphan.

  • Server-side postbacks with query-style URLs and macros for click id, payout, status and transaction id.
  • Duplicate protection and retroactive status or payout updates.
  • Clickless conversions attributed by sub-id when a click id was never stored.
  • Manual import for networks that can only give you a CSV at the end of the day.

Questions

The things buyers ask first.

What exactly does AAA Tracker do?

It sits between your traffic and your offers. It decides which offer or landing page each visitor sees, records the click, records the conversion when the network posts it back, pulls your ad spend in, and reports profit per campaign, source, offer and placement.

Will it work with my traffic sources and networks?

Yes. Sources and networks carry their own pass-through macros, so campaign, placement, keyword and sub-id data is captured without hand-built parameters. If a source isn't in the list, adding one takes a minute.

How does it handle iOS and cookie restrictions?

Clicks are recorded server-side and conversions come back as postbacks or server-side conversion API calls, so attribution doesn't depend on a third-party cookie surviving.

Is my data separate from other customers?

Yes. Every record is scoped to your workspace, and platform tokens are encrypted at rest. See Security for the detail.

Do I need to be technical to set it up?

No. You add a source, an offer and a campaign, then copy the tracking link. Landing pages can be uploaded or pulled from a URL, and ad platforms connect through OAuth rather than a pasted token.

What does it cost?

It's free while we're onboarding design partners, with no per-event metering. See Pricing.
