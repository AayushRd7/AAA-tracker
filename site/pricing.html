Every plan

What's included in every plan.

Capability is not gated on the tier you pick. Whether you are running one offer or fifty client accounts, the routing engine and the reporting underneath it are the same.

  • Workspace isolation — every click, conversion and report is scoped to your workspace.
  • Unlimited campaigns, offers and landers — no tiers by volume.
  • 30+ routing filters with AND/OR groups, caps, dayparting and fallbacks.
  • Postbacks with de-duplication — retried sales never post twice.
  • Reports and logs — breakdowns, saved views and the raw records to debug a discrepancy.
  • Anti-fraud scoring — a fraud score on every click, plus bot filtering and shields.
  • No per-event metering — clicks, conversions and postbacks are not counted or billed.
The fine print, up front

What we don't do.

Tracking is the one piece of your stack you cannot afford to be surprised by. These are the billing behaviours we have deliberately left out.

  • No per-click or per-conversion billing — a good month does not cost more because it worked.
  • No feature paywall on the routing engine — filters, caps and fallbacks are core, not an upgrade.
  • No charge for seats while you evaluate — bring the people who need to see the numbers.
  • No lock-in on your own data — export it or delete it on demand, whenever you want.
Questions

What buyers ask about pricing.

How much does it cost?

Nothing right now. AAA Tracker is free while we onboard design partners, with the full platform switched on. We have not published prices yet because billing is not live — when usage-based plans and self-serve billing arrive, you will see them here before anything changes for an existing workspace.

Is there a limit on clicks or events?

No. There is no click ceiling, conversion ceiling or postback ceiling during early access. The platform does not meter or charge per click, conversion or postback, so a campaign that scales does not quietly move you into a bigger bill.

What happens when billing launches?

Existing early-access workspaces get advance notice before any paid plan applies, and they keep their data. Nothing is deleted, and nothing is retroactively charged. You will be able to decide with the numbers in front of you.

Can I bring my own domain?

Yes. Tracking links can run on your own domain, and domains can be grouped so each client or brand keeps its own. Your ad platform sees your domain, not ours.

How do I migrate from a spreadsheet or another tracker?

Start with one live campaign. We onboard you personally — source, offer, campaign and postback — and you can compare what AAA Tracker records against your current numbers before moving anything else across.

How do I get help?

You talk to the people who build the product. During early access that is a direct line, not a ticket queue. Book a walkthrough and we will set the first campaign up with you.
