Questions

What buyers ask about pricing.

How much does it cost?

Start with a 14-day free trial. Paid plans: Starter $49 for 500,000 tracked events a month, Growth $149 for 3 million, Scale $499 for 20 million, and Enterprise on custom terms.

What counts as a tracked event?

One visit, one click or one conversion. One meter — cost sync calls, postback retries, API reads and seats are never counted against you.

What happens if I go over my plan?

Tracking never switches off. We email you, you decide, and any upgrade is prorated from the day you moved. There is no automatic overage charge.

Can I bring my own domain?

Yes. Tracking links can run on your own domain, and domains can be grouped so each client or brand keeps its own. Your ad platform sees your domain, not ours.

How do I migrate from a spreadsheet or another tracker?

Start with one live campaign. We onboard you personally — source, offer, campaign and postback — and you can compare what AAA Tracker records against your current numbers before moving anything else across.

How do I get help?

You talk to the people who build the product — a direct line, not a ticket queue. Book a walkthrough and we’ll set the first campaign up with you.
