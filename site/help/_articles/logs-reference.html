Six trails, one shape

The Logs section keeps six separate event trails, one per sidebar entry. Every page has the same controls: a From and To date range, filters for that log, Refresh, Export CSV, a Columns picker and a Preset dropdown where you can Save filters and reuse them later.

A log can also be opened pre-filtered by a link. Put a filter name in the query string — for example ?click_id=YOUR-CLICK-ID — and the matching field is filled in when the page loads. The Health centre uses this to jump from a failed delivery straight to the row behind it. Copy a click id from Logs → Clicks and paste it into any other log's URL to follow one visitor across all six.

Clicks

Every click the tracker received. Columns: Time, Click id, IP, Country, Device, Status, URL, Bot. Filters: Country, Device, OS, Status, and a free-text Search over URL, IP and visitor id.

Open it when you need to confirm a click actually arrived — a campaign link hit lands here first — or to copy a click id for a manual postback test. The Bot flag tells you at a glance whether the hit was filtered as automated.

Conversions

The inbound conversion records as stored. Columns: Time, Click id, Status, Campaign, Offer, Payout, Revenue, Transaction. Filters: Status, Click id and Campaign id.

Open it when a postback returned 200 and you want to see the conversion row it created, or to confirm that an unknown click id became an unattributed row. This list is read-only; to change a status, payout or delete a row, use the conversion log on Reports.

S2S postbacks

Every inbound server-to-server call to /pb, accepted or not. Columns: Time, Click id, Status, Result, Payout, Transaction, Source IP, Reason, Raw URL. Filters: Click id, Status, Result, and a free-text Search over URL, reason and IP.

The Result column is the diagnosis: accepted, duplicate, rejected or a rule/block outcome, with the Reason spelling out why. Open it when a network says it posted but revenue did not move — the reason tells you whether the call was refused before anything was written, or removed by the duplicate guard. See Postback not recorded.

Internal postbacks

The outbound delivery attempts this tracker sent onward, such as conversions pushed to an ad platform. Columns: Time, Click id, Platform, Pixel, Event, Status, HTTP, Outcome, Attempts, Response. Filters: Click id, Event, Conversion status and Outcome.

The HTTP column and the clipped Response body are the delivery receipt: they show what the receiving platform answered. Open it when a conversion is missing downstream — the tracker recorded it, but the send may have failed or been retried. See Sending conversions to Meta (CAPI) for one platform's path.

Click forwarding

Every redirect decision a click went through. Columns: Time, Click id, Campaign, Flow, Schema, Offer, Status, Destination, Reason. Filters: Click id, Campaign id, Status, and a free-text Search over reason and destination.

Open it when a visitor reached the wrong destination or no redirect at all. The Status and Reason show which routing outcome was taken — a normal redirect, a fallback, a cap, an out-of-schedule flow or a blocked request — and the Destination is the URL the visitor was sent to.

Cost updates

Retroactive cost changes applied from Reports. Columns: Time, Username, Campaign, From, To, Cost, Rows. Filters: Username and Campaign id.

Open it when cost for a period changed unexpectedly or you want to know who last touched it. Each row records who applied the update, the window it covered, the new per-click cost and how many rows were rewritten.

Follow one click across every log

Copy the click id from Logs → Clicks, then open another log with ?click_id=… appended to its URL. The filter is seeded for you, so Clicks → Click forwarding → S2S postbacks → Internal postbacks read as one story.
