Security & anti-fraud

Your data, your numbers, nobody else’s.

Tracking touches your spend, your traffic and your conversion data. This page is the honest version of what protects it — the controls that exist, what they do, and where the line sits between a practice and a certificate.

Workspace isolation

One workspace, one filtered view of the world.

Every record in AAA Tracker — campaigns, clicks, conversions, offers, landers and saved credentials — carries the workspace it belongs to, and every query is filtered by that workspace. There is no shared pool your numbers fall into.

  • Scoped records — campaigns, clicks, conversions, offers and tokens are all bound to a workspace.
  • Filtered queries — reads and writes are filtered by the workspace the request belongs to.
  • Roles & permissions — team members get a role with section-level access.
  • Nothing visible by default — invited users see only what they were granted.

Credentials

Tokens are secrets, and they’re treated like it.

Connecting an ad platform means storing something that can spend money. Ad-platform tokens are encrypted at rest and decrypted only when a job needs them — never held in the open on a request.

  • Encrypted at rest — ad-platform tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Decrypted on demand — only when a job needs the token to run.
  • Masked in responses — tokens come back as a masked value, never in full.
  • Not in settings export — exporting your settings does not carry your tokens out.
  • Deployment credentials live in the environment — platform-level secrets are configuration, not a field a customer fills in.

Conversion endpoints

Endpoints that only accept what you accept.

A postback is the most valuable URL in your stack — it is the thing that reports revenue. It should not accept traffic from anyone who finds it.

Secret key or IP allowlist

Require a secret on the postback URL, restrict callers to an IP allowlist, or both.

Rejected with 403

Requests that fail those checks are refused outright — not recorded as conversions.

De-duplication

A 60-second window plus transaction ids stop a retried postback from becoming a second sale.

Retroactive, audited

Status and payout updates after the fact move every report with them, and the change is logged.

Anti-fraud

Filter the traffic that was never going to convert.

Bots don’t buy. Every click is scored and the response to bad traffic is yours to choose — silently absorb it, block it, or route it somewhere harmless.

0–100 fraud score

Every click carries a score built from proxy, datacenter, headless-browser and behavioural signals.

Verified-crawler detection

Real Googlebot and friends are recognised and not punished; fake ones are.

Blocklists & shields

Editable global user-agent and IP blocklists, plus per-campaign allowlist shields.

Configurable response

Blank page, 404, or fall through to a safe flow — your call, per campaign.

Privacy

Collect less. Keep it for a defined window. Let people opt out.

  • IP anonymisation — addresses can be truncated at collection time.
  • Retention window — you define how long click-level data is kept.
  • Opt-out endpoint — a first-party route visitors can use to stop tracking.

Full detail on what is collected and how long it is kept lives in the privacy policy.

Accountability

Lock the front door, then write down who walked through it.

  • Audit trail — who changed what, and when.
  • What is stored — clicks, conversions and cost records, plus the identifiers your tracking sends.
  • Your controls — export or delete on demand, no permission slip required.
Questions

The security questions buyers ask first.

Is my data separate from other customers?

Yes. Every record — campaigns, clicks, conversions, offers and saved credentials — is scoped to your workspace, and every query is filtered by it.

What happens to my ad-platform tokens?

They are encrypted at rest, decrypted only when a job needs them, masked in API responses, and excluded from settings exports.

Can I lock down the postback endpoint?

Yes — a secret key, an IP allowlist, or both. Calls that fail the check are rejected with a 403 and never recorded.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. Your tracking data is processed to run the service for you. The privacy policy states it in plain language.

Can I run it entirely on my own infrastructure?

Yes — that is what the MIT-licensed self-hosted edition is for. Your server, your database, your uptime. See Start free.
