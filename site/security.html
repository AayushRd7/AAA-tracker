Security & anti-fraud

Your data, your numbers, nobody else's.

Tracking touches your spend, your traffic and your conversion data. This page is the honest version of what protects it — the controls that exist, what they do, and where the line sits between a practice and a certificate.

Workspace isolation

One workspace, one filtered view of the world.

Every record in AAA Tracker — campaigns, clicks, conversions, offers, landers and saved credentials — carries the workspace it belongs to, and every query is filtered by that workspace. There is no shared pool your numbers fall into.

  • Scoped records — campaigns, clicks, conversions, offers and tokens are all bound to a workspace.
  • Filtered queries — reads and writes are filtered by the workspace the request belongs to.
  • Roles & permissions — team members get a role with section-level access.
  • Invited users see only what they were granted — nothing is visible by default.

Credentials

Tokens are secrets, and they're treated like it.

Connecting an ad platform means storing something that can spend money. Ad-platform tokens are encrypted at rest and decrypted only when a job needs them — never held in the open on a request.

  • Encrypted at rest — ad-platform tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Decrypted on demand — only when a job needs the token to run.
  • Masked in responses — tokens come back as a masked value, never in full.
  • Not in settings export — exporting your settings does not carry your tokens out.
  • Deployment credentials live in the environment — platform-level secrets are configuration, not a field a customer fills in.
Postback protection

Conversion endpoints that only accept what you accept.

An open postback URL is an invitation: anyone who guesses it can write a fake conversion into your reports. Inbound endpoints can be locked down the same way you'd lock down any other API.

Secret key or IP allowlist

A conversion endpoint can require a secret key, restrict the source to an IP allowlist, or enforce both. You choose per endpoint.

Rejected with 403

A postback that arrives without valid authentication is rejected with 403 and recorded — not silently dropped in.

De-duplication

A retried postback is de-duplicated inside a 60-second window and by transaction id, so one sale stays one sale.

Bot & fraud defence

Filter the traffic that was never going to convert.

Paid traffic brings bots with it. AAA Tracker scores every click and gives you the switches to act on what it finds — globally, per campaign, and permanently once a visitor outs itself.

0–100 fraud score

Every click is scored from proxy, datacenter-ISP and headless-browser signals, so you can set the threshold you're comfortable with.

Verified-crawler detection

Built-in detection recognises verified crawlers, so search and preview bots don't distort your click numbers.

Blocklists & shields

Editable global user-agent and IP blocklists, plus per-campaign shields with IP, referrer and user-agent allowlists.

Configurable response

When a shield matches, choose what the visitor gets: a blank page, a 404, or a fall-through to a safe flow.

Privacy controls

Collect less, keep it for a defined window, and let people opt out.

These are switches on the workspace, not promises in a document. Each one changes what the tracker stores or how long it stores it.

IP anonymisation

Anonymise the IP address recorded with a click, so the tracking record keeps its use without keeping the full address.

Retention window

A configurable data-retention window with automatic pruning — old click and conversion records are trimmed on schedule.

Opt-out endpoint

An opt-out endpoint suppresses further tracking for a visitor who asks not to be followed.

Account security

Lock the front door, then write down who walked through it.

Access to the data is the other half of the job. Sign-in is protected, and every change leaves a trace you can read later.

  • Two-factor authentication — TOTP-based 2FA on your account.
  • Login IP allowlist — restrict sign-in to the addresses you expect.
  • Session cookies — sessions are carried in cookies, not in URLs.
  • Audit log — records who changed what, and from where.

Data handling

What we store, for how long, and who can take it out.

Rather than a badge, here is the plain description. You stay the controller of your campaign data; AAA Tracker processes it on your behalf.

What is stored

Click, conversion and cost records, together with the identifiers you send us — click id, sub-ids, campaign and placement data, and the conversion values your network posts back. That is the data the reports are built from.

How long

For as long as your configured retention window allows. Once a record falls outside it, it is pruned automatically. You can clear tracking history on demand before that window elapses.

Your controls

Export your settings at any time, clear tracking history on demand, and tune retention, anonymisation and opt-out to match your own policy. Credentials are excluded from the settings export.

A data processing agreement is available on request. Tell us what your legal or procurement team needs and we'll work through it with you — get in touch.

Questions

The security questions buyers ask first.

Where is my data stored?

Your clicks, conversions, cost records and the identifiers you send are stored in the deployment that serves your workspace. If you need to know the region for a procurement review, ask us and we'll confirm it for your setup rather than guess.

Who on my team can see what?

Access is role-based and section-scoped. An owner can see everything; an editor might get campaigns and reports; a viewer might only get reports. Invited users see exactly what their grant allows and nothing else, because every query is filtered by workspace and permission.

What happens if a postback has no valid key?

It is rejected with a 403 and recorded in the log, so you can see that something tried and was turned away. If the endpoint requires an IP allowlist as well, a request from outside it is rejected the same way.

Can I delete tracking history?

Yes. You can clear tracking history on demand, and your retention window prunes old records automatically. Exporting your settings and clearing history are both self-serve.

Is my data used to train anything or shared?

No. Your campaign data is processed to run your tracking and reporting, not to train models and not sold or shared as a data set. Each workspace's records are isolated, and platform credentials stay encrypted and outside settings exports.

Tell us how you buy traffic and what your compliance team needs. We'll walk through the controls that apply to you — isolation, postback auth, retention, agreements — on a call.
